    How this works

    Each company is matched to its public job board (Comeet, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters or Workday), and a crawler reads those boards every week. Only jobs located in Israel are kept, and only technical roles: software, data and AI, algorithms, security, DevOps and cloud, QA, embedded, chip design, IT and technical product jobs. Sales, marketing, finance, HR and operations roles are left out. Job types and levels are worked out from the job title, so a few may be misfiled. "Large" means about 1,000 or more employees; sizes are estimates. "First seen" is the first check that found the job, not the employer's posting date.
