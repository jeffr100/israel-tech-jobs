    How this works

    Each company is matched to its public job board (Comeet, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters or Workday), and a crawler reads those boards every morning at 6:00 Israel time. Only jobs located in Israel are kept, and only technology roles: software, data and AI, security, DevOps, QA, embedded, chip design, electronics and hardware, optics and systems engineering, mechanical and manufacturing engineering, medtech and life-science R&D, technical sales and solutions engineering, design and UX, IT and technical product jobs. Sales, marketing, finance, HR and operations roles are left out. Job types and seniority are worked out from the job title, so a few may be misfiled. "Large" means about 1,000 or more employees. Dates are the employer's posting date when the job board gives one, otherwise the first day this site saw the job.

    The "not on LinkedIn or Indeed" filter uses a separate check that searches those sites for each job. It turns on once that check has run (see the README).
